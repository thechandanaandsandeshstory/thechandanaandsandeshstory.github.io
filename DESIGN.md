# Silver at blue hour

## Direction
An illuminated Kannada invitation unfolding into a blue-hour courtyard. The invitation's cobalt ink and silver foliage supply the identity. First viewport: immersive botanical blue paper, a fine architectural arch, the couple's names set as the invitation itself, and the wedding date immediately visible. The opening is the signature animated moment; event information stays still and legible.

The grounded explorations were silver invitation foil, jasmine garlands, Malnad landscapes, silk weaving, courtyard architecture, Kannada book typography, and a blue-hour courtyard invitation. Seed reference: seed:65b59e105aea6c0c; seventh direction. The user instructed building after the invitation review; no additional concept approval is required. Code-led implementation. The illuminated-initial challenger contributes the discipline of a framing margin, without importing its European motifs; cloud-edge contributes restraint outside the focal artwork. Kiln and jazz sleeve forms lack the invitation's cultural connection.
